Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · ElmParkTech

1. Overview

ElmParkTech builds mobile applications and digital tools with a commitment to user privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled across our website (elmparktech.github.io) and our published mobile applications unless an app provides its own dedicated privacy policy.

2. Information We Collect

Our static website does not require user accounts, login credentials, or personal form submissions. For our mobile applications, we minimize data collection and only request device permissions strictly necessary for core app functionality.

  • User-Provided Information: If you contact us via email for support, we use your email address and message solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Reports: Where enabled, anonymized crash logs and performance diagnostics may be collected to improve stability.

3. Third-Party Services

Individual applications may integrate trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google Play Services, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob). Any third-party service used by a specific app is disclosed in that app's dedicated privacy policy and Google Play Data Safety section.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as new applications are released. For any questions or data-related requests, please contact us at support@elmparktech.com.
