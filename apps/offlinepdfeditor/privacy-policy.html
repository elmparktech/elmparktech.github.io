PDFPoint Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 · Published by ElmParkTech

1. App & Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application PDFPoint (package ID: net.vertexapps.offlinepdfeditor), published by ElmParkTech.

  • No Account Required: PDFPoint does not require user registration, login, or account creation to use its features.
  • Contact: For privacy questions or data requests, email support@elmparktech.com.

2. On-Device Document & File Processing

All core PDF operations—including viewing, editing, merging, splitting, compressing, signing, redacting, OCR, and format conversion—run locally on your device. Your PDF files and document contents are not uploaded to developer servers.

  • Scoped File Access: Files are opened through Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) and saved using MediaStore or SAF. PDFPoint does not declare broad legacy storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • Local Preferences: App preferences (such as theme, language, recent/favorite tools, and settings) are stored locally on your device using Jetpack DataStore. Android cloud backup is disabled (allowBackup="false").

3. Usage Analytics (Microsoft Clarity)

PDFPoint integrates Microsoft Clarity to understand general app usability (such as tap interactions, generic screen navigation, heatmaps, and session replays) and improve interface quality.

  • Screen Masking: Personal and document-bearing screens (including the PDF viewer, file names, AI chat, and tool screens) are masked. Only the Tools list and Settings screens are recorded unmasked.
  • No Personal Text or File Names: Screen names logged to analytics are generic route or tool identifiers only—never document file names or typed user text.
  • Consent-Aware Recording: In regions where consent is required (such as the EU/UK), Clarity recording remains paused unless you grant consent through the app's consent prompt.
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement: Learn more at privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

4. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

PDFPoint uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage regional privacy consent.

  • Data Used for Ads: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, ad interaction events, and diagnostic information to serve, personalize (where consented), and measure ads.
  • Managing Consent: Users in regions requiring consent (such as the EEA/UK) are prompted via Google UMP and can update or withdraw their ad consent choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Google Privacy Policy: Learn more at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. AI Features (Online Chat Help & Optional Offline AI)

PDFPoint offers optional AI-assisted features that you control:

  • AI Chat Help (Optional, Toggleable): When enabled and used, PDFPoint sends only your typed prompt text (up to 250 characters) and a randomly generated per-install identifier (X-Install-Id, used strictly for rate limiting with no personal data) over HTTPS to the AI help server. PDF files and document contents are never transmitted.
  • Offline AI Summaries (Optional Download): With your explicit consent, you may download an on-device language model (Qwen3-0.6B-int4, approximately 347 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) using a foreground service with a progress notification. Once downloaded, AI summarization runs entirely offline on your device and can be deleted anytime from Settings.

6. Google ML Kit & Web-to-PDF

  • Document Scanner, OCR, Language ID & Translate: PDFPoint uses Google ML Kit APIs. Document scanning uses Google Play services' scanner UI (no CAMERA permission is declared by PDFPoint). OCR and translation run on-device, with translation language packs downloaded on demand when you choose to translate.
  • Web to PDF: When you enter a web URL in the Web-to-PDF tool, the app uses internet access to load that page and render it into a PDF locally on your device.

7. Android Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Used for Google AdMob ads, Microsoft Clarity analytics (when active/consented), Web-to-PDF page loading, optional AI chat help, and downloading optional AI or translation models.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Keeps long-running on-device AI processing or model downloads running reliably in the background.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays optional progress notifications for background AI processing and model downloads.

8. Data Retention, Deletion & Children's Privacy

  • User Control & Deletion: You can delete PDFs directly in the app or your device's file manager, remove downloaded offline AI models in Settings, or clear app data / uninstall PDFPoint to erase all local preferences and cached models.
  • Third-Party Retention: Data processed by Google (AdMob) or Microsoft (Clarity) is governed by their respective privacy and retention policies.
  • Children's Privacy: PDFPoint is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us at support@elmparktech.com for prompt removal.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
