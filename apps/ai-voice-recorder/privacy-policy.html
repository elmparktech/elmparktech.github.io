AI Voice Recorder Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Published by ElmParkTech

1. App & Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application AI Voice Recorder, published by ElmParkTech.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or create an account to record audio or transcribe locally.
  • Contact: For privacy questions or data requests, email support@elmparktech.com.

2. On-Device Audio Recording, Storage & Transcription

AI Voice Recorder is built with an offline-first architecture. Audio recording, playback, audio editing, and speech-to-text transcription (powered by on-device Whisper / ONNX models) run locally on your device.

  • Audio Never Uploaded for Transcription: Your voice recordings and audio files are never uploaded to developer servers for transcription.
  • Local Storage & No Cloud Backup: Recordings, transcripts, bookmarks, and app preferences are stored locally on your device (Room database and Jetpack DataStore). Android cloud backup is disabled (allowBackup="false", fullBackupContent="false").
  • No Broad Storage, Contacts, Location, or Phone Permissions: Audio imported via system share (audio/*) or exported from the app uses Android's scoped FileProvider and system pickers.
  • Optional Biometric App Lock: If you enable app lock, Android's system biometric prompt (USE_BIOMETRIC) verifies your identity locally; the app never accesses or stores biometric data.

3. Speech Model Downloads

To transcribe speech offline, you can download open-source Whisper speech recognition models (such as tiny, base, or small quantized models) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) on demand.

  • Downloads occur over HTTPS only when initiated by you and run via Android's WorkManager foreground service so progress persists reliably.
  • Downloaded models are stored locally on your device and can be deleted at any time from Settings.

4. Optional AI Text Summaries

Alongside offline smart notes, AI Voice Recorder may offer an optional cloud AI summary feature. This feature only runs when explicitly triggered by you after you agree:

  • Transcript Text Only: Only the transcribed text and detected/selected language are sent over HTTPS to the summary server (ai-api.vertexapps.net). Your audio recordings are never uploaded.
  • Rate-Limit Identifier: Requests include a randomly generated per-install identifier (X-Install-Id) used solely for rate limiting, containing no personal information.
  • Local Storage: Generated summaries are saved locally on your device and can be deleted individually at any time.

5. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Free tiers of AI Voice Recorder display advertisements via Google AdMob and manage regional privacy consent using Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

  • Consent-First Initialization: The AdMob SDK is initialized only after checking regional consent status.
  • Data Collected by AdMob: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, ad interaction events, and diagnostics to serve, personalize (where consented), and measure ads.
  • Manage Ad Choices: Users in regions requiring consent (such as the EEA/UK) can review or change their ad consent choices anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Google Privacy Policy: Learn more at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

AI Voice Recorder uses Google Play Billing for optional Pro subscriptions, lifetime upgrades, and ad-removal purchases. All payment transactions, billing details, and subscription renewals are processed securely by Google Play. ElmParkTech does not collect or store your payment card or financial details.

7. Analytics

AI Voice Recorder does not integrate third-party behavioral analytics or session-replay SDKs.

8. Android Permissions Used

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Captures audio from your microphone when you start a recording.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING: Supports user-initiated recording with the screen off, lock-screen playback controls, model downloads, and offline transcription jobs.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays ongoing recording, playback, download, and transcription progress notifications.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps the CPU active during an active recording when the screen is turned off.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Enables optional local app lock via fingerprint, face, or device screen lock.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used for downloading offline transcription models, optional user-triggered AI text summaries, Google AdMob ads, and Google Play Billing.

9. Data Retention, Deletion & Children's Privacy

  • User Control & Deletion: You can delete individual recordings, transcripts, AI summaries, or downloaded speech models directly inside the app, or clear app data / uninstall the app to remove all local files and preferences.
  • Third-Party Retention: Any data processed by Google (AdMob or Google Play Billing) is governed by Google's privacy and retention policies.
  • Children's Privacy: AI Voice Recorder is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact support@elmparktech.com if you believe a child has provided personal data.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
